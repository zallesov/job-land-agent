# Job Fit Assessment Pattern

When asked to evaluate a job posting against Zall's profile/CV, produce:

## Structure

1. **Brief company intro** — what they do, stage, size, funding, team quality signals
2. **Requirements table** comparing each requirement against the CV

| Requirement | Fit Assessment |
|---|---|
| Requirement 1 | Strong/Weak/Neutral — with specific evidence from CV |
| Requirement 2 | ... |
| ... | ... |

3. **Bottom-line verdict** — one of:
   - **Skip** — misaligned with Zall's profile; focus elsewhere
   - **Stretch apply** — possible but requires CV retooling or story pivoting
   - **Strong fit** — directly aligned, recommend applying

4. **Next steps** — what action to take (apply, skip, refer, etc.)

## Key heuristics

- Zall's core story is Senior/Cloud/AI Engineer. Roles in product analytics, growth, data analysis, or management that don't leverage engineering depth are weak fits.
- Strong signals: AI/ML infrastructure, cloud architecture, systems engineering, distributed systems.
- Weak signals: pure data analysis, product analytics leadership, growth/marketing analytics — unless the user expressed intent to pivot.
- When table cells are densely packed (multi-line), keep table borders sparse for terminal readability — don't add heavy separator rows between each line.
