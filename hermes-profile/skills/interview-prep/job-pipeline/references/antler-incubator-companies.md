## Research Tips

- Antler blog has cohort reveal articles (e.g., `/blog/meet-the-8-new-startups-backed-by-antler-in-its-second-berlin-cohort`)
- Blog posts often truncate company details — portfolio page is more complete
- Crunchbase/Glassdoor/LinkedIn still useful for individual company deep-dives
- Antler is pre-seed/seed stage investor — most companies are very early stage (1-25 employees)
- Berlin cohorts run ~2x per year, ~50 founders per cohort, ~8 teams funded
